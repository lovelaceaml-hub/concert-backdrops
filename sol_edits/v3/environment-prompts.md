# Sol V3 environment art

These four environment paintings were generated with ChatGPT's image tool for
this independent version. They are used as projected paintings with local camera
drift and atmospheric distortion, behind real locally rendered 3D bodies, cloth,
petals, contact shadows and light. They are not generated video clips. No
ElevenLabs media is used. The exact source PNGs are archived separately in Drive;
`assets.json` records their hashes. Do not commit the images.

The direction was warm, radiant, richly detailed and welcoming. No people, text,
logos or icons were requested, so the local engine retains control of acting,
exact campaign wording and logo geometry.

## Garden

A heavenly garden of peace above an immense sea of sculpted luminous clouds at
sunrise, viewed from a pale travertine and champagne marble terrace. Slender ivory
columns and tall classical arches with gold filigree on the far right. A flowering
magnolia on the right third, distant waterfalls falling into cloud, rich azure sky
and warm champagne sunbeams. Center and left open to a vast cloud vista, distant
mountains and soaring spires. Foreground terrace across the bottom fifth. Detailed
physical materials and cloud volumes, poetic fantasy film realism. No white
clipping, people, text or glitter overlay.

## Reconciliation

A luminous white marble garden terrace with champagne gold inlay at sunrise after
rain. Wet reflective marble across the foreground quarter. Flowering almond trees
at both edges, layered cloud valley and sunlight opening through pale blue rain
clouds. An arched bridge crosses the valley toward distant white spires and
mountains. Warm cream, cornflower blue and champagne. Open floor at center right
for the animated people. Tender, radiant and peaceful, immense atmospheric depth.
No people, writing or dark silhouettes.

## Cathedral

An immense open-air heavenly cathedral above clouds. Exquisitely carved ivory
marble columns, gold capitals and soaring luminous vaulted arches receding to an
open cloud vista. Champagne gold, warm cream and soft blue. Magnolia flowers frame
the outer right. Polished marble floor in the foreground quarter, reflecting the
vaults and golden inlay. Open center-right for people, lower left open for words.
Beautiful atmospheric shafts, huge scale, warm luminous shadow detail, no crosses,
statues, people, icons, writing or clipped whites.

## Ascent

A view from above a vast three dimensional sea of luminous sculpted clouds. A
champagne sunrise shines through a colossal opening in pearl and peach clouds,
azure sky and radial volumetric sunbeams. Realistic cloud volumes to the horizon,
immense scale and sacred peace. Near cloud banks at the lower corners frame a
clear passage toward the sun. No architecture, people, letters, logos, stars or
sparkle overlay. Retain highlight detail.
