## Choices made against the design text (owner rules first)

- No "cold pictures of love" in the water (river, cut, hollow of darkness): the owner's rule is warm, never dark, cold or eerie. The verse 1 water stays calm and starlit.
- No vast hands of light in the dawn: the owner found the hands at the end of the promo "extremely weird"; Heavenly Mother's love is the star that became the seed and the sunrise itself.
- The traveler is a woman, so every touch (hand on the lantern, hand on the shoulder) is between women.
- Nobody dissolves into sparks: the young woman and the four at the end fade softly into light, whole and radiant.
- The descent under the snow is a gentle glide toward the seed's glow with its roots of light seen through the ice (the camera stays calm for the performers).
- The deepest night is a deep starry blue with warm lights, never black.
