# 05 God Bless You!: acting plan per expression (version 2)

Campaign meaning (heavenlymotherslove.org checklist and the Mother's Love app, read by a helper this session):
- **Encouragement (7)** "I'll pray (or root) for you. Everything will go well.": sincere support and hope for someone in a hard moment; praying for them, cheering them on.
- **Consideration (8)** "Can I give you a hand with anything?": first noticing the people around you, then offering practical help.
- **Respect (6)** "I'd love to hear more about your thoughts.": giving another person's view full attention and asking them to share more, especially when opinions differ. Here a provisional exception (DESIGN-SYSTEM 12a.2): the brother pauses and truly listens to his sister.

People: four warm gold glitter people of light (Claude Final v1 bodies), whole and radiant, clothed in light (coat, tunic, dress, robe), no facial features, every grain painted gold with a gentle warm light inside the shape. Touch only between two people of the same gender. Calm wide framing from low over the water; the people stand between the words and the calm bottom third.
