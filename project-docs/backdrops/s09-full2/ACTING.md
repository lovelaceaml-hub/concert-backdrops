# Song 09 "Let's Save the World": acting plan (backdrop version 2)

All people are warm gold glitter people (radiant, whole, clothed in garments of light, soft luminous faceless heads). Every touching moment is between two people of the same gender. Heavenly Mother only from behind. No person is mirrored in the water (keeps the lower third calm). Motions are eased (the engine's refined acting: master_acting, gestures, pairs).

## The expression: Encouragement (star 7, relit)

- **Lyric line:** verse 2, line 3 (2:55.5 to 3:08): the saints call out to us in hardship and say glory waits for us. That is the act the campaign calls Encouragement: sincere support for someone in a hard moment, so they know they are not alone ("I'll pray (or root) for you. Everything will go well."). The only expression placed by the design.
- **Acted by:** two women in the present day, warm gold glitter look. The tired one kneels on the near shore with head bowed (2:54 onward). Her friend comes, kneels beside her (2:55.6 to 2:57.4) and rests her right hand on the tired one's shoulder (2:57.4 to 3:07.2), the PHASEB 6 table's "a hand on the shoulder of someone kneeling". As the words complete the tired one lifts her head and turns to her friend (3:03 to 3:07.6); her glow strengthens and the water round them goes still. They rise together (3:07.3 to 3:09.6) and watch the light go up into star 7 (3:09.6 to 3:16).
- **Framing:** low, almost eye level, horizon at mid frame, the camera still during the reading. The words in the upper middle (block centred about a third of the way down), the pair below them (about 0.45 to 0.67 of the frame height), the arch across the sky above. Nothing else crosses the reading box (lights inside it are dimmed); no other text or burst.
- **Symbol in the world:** praying hands traced by violet sparks in the sky over the saints' lamps (2:51 to 2:56), before the words.
- **Hearts:** small violet hearts of light drift up off the words as they leave for star 7 (3:07.6 to 3:14).
- **Light:** violet flames carry the saints' light across the water from their time to ours (2:54.8 to 2:58.6) and gather into the words.
