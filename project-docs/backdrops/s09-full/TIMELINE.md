## Differences from the design doc (and why)

- The design's operator cues per movement are replaced by one continuous video (owner decision 2026-11-18).
- The design's last 15 to 20 s dusk (deep blue) would make the end dark; the hold here only warms to a low rose-gold sun, so it stays warm and never dark. Song 10 can still open from a warm low sun.
- The design's "words spelled by people's lights" and "forge" effects are simplified: the fragments use the approved R3 pen of light; "hardships as honor" is in warm forge orange.
- The fishermen's net slipping away and the boats' figures casting the net are suggested, not acted in detail.
