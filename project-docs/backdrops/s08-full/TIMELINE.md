## Differences from the design document (and why)

- The design was timed on the 2:38 sung guide; the orchestral recording is 3:29 with a different shape, so every moment above is placed on the measured sections.
- Expressions are held about 10 s (backdrop rule: 8 to 10 s), not 5 to 6 s.
- The opening title sits in the upper middle and then gives way to the approved Revision 3 signature at the bottom left (small), rather than resting at the top left.
- Not built (would need new engine work): the swan, the olive tree, the outline glimpses of everyday kindness in the congregation's lights, the cherubim and pomegranates in the embroidery, the city of light beyond the curtain. The curtain opens on radiant light only (open question 2 in the design answered conservatively).
