# 07 May No One Ever Feel Lonely: Video Design
**Concert:** The Words of Mother's Love Healing Concert (LV Zion), Sunday December 6, 2026, 4:30 PM, 6720 W Sahara Ave, Las Vegas. Performed live by a male duet or quartet with choir and orchestra, about 3:58.
**Song:** 07 May No One Ever Feel Lonely (Appendix 17). **Recording:** 07 May No One Ever Feel Lonely.mp3 (studio, 4:06).
**Status:** revision 3 (continuity pass), November 13, 2026, for Anthony's review. Follows the decisions adopted provisionally while Anthony is away (DESIGN-SYSTEM.md section 12a): star history, the 3:58 live length, the poster's concert title, a relit star shown as a brief flare and one new thin ring, operator-cued sections that each end in a calm hold. THE MESSAGE stays at the center: the Words of Mother's Love bring peace. A 1 minute promo has been made from this design (section 15).
## 1. The idea in one paragraph: "No Light Left Alone"
The song is three of Heavenly Mother's earnest wishes, each answered by Her child's promise. The video shows how that promise is kept: with two of Mother's words, spoken in ordinary life. An elderly traveler of light walks a lakeside path alone, carrying a heavy bundle, and people pass by. One person stops and says "How are you?", then "Can I give you a hand with anything?" Peace settles over the whole lake. The one who was helped becomes a helper, and the kindness spreads from person to person until every lonely light in the world is found and the whole family walks home to the kingdom of heaven together, led by Heavenly Mother, seen only from behind. The audience should leave knowing two of Mother's words, when to use them on an ordinary day, and that using them brings peace on earth and leads us home.
## 2. What the campaign teaches this song
- **The tagline is the picture.** The campaign's own line is "Where a mother's love reaches, peace settles." In this video, every time one of Mother's words is spoken, a slow golden ring of stillness spreads across the lake and smooths every ripple. Peace is something you can see settle.
- **Greeting is "the first phrase to peace".** The campaign describes it as a heartfelt hello to the people we see every day or pass casually: the neighbor in the elevator, a friend in the hallway, the people who look after the neighborhood. It is also the expression people practice most. So our greeting happens on an ordinary path, to someone others walked past.
- **Consideration "begins with paying attention to those around you".** The campaign's own first example is offering a hand to an older person carrying something heavy, and it says the smallest act of kindness can become someone's greatest encouragement. That is exactly our scene: noticing, then lifting the load.
- **Extend your hand first.** The campaign asks us to reach out first rather than wait. Our helper is the one who stops, turns back and kneels.
- **Peacemakers spread peace.** The campaign calls everyone a peacemaker and speaks of warm words spreading through homes, schools, workplaces and whole cities, and of reconnecting distant hearts across more than a hundred countries. So in this video the helped one becomes a helper, and lights from far shores join the journey home: one hello grows into a whole world at peace.
- **Family and home.** The campaign centers on families and neighbors (its short films are set at a family meal, in an elevator and in an office). This song ends with the whole family going home together with Mother, which is where every small word of love is leading.

## 4. The world of this song
- **Home scene, made more beautiful:** the shared still lake at blue dusk (this song's own look, DESIGN-SYSTEM.md section 6: twilight blue, coral and lantern gold, the first stars coming out one by one, soft mist over the water, and a deep view down a long path home), the long mountains and their perfect reflection, warm particles drifting like slow fireflies, and the warm star resting on the horizon. In this song the horizon star is Heavenly Mother's presence: Her wishes come from it, and at the end She walks toward it.
- **The lakeside path and village:** along the far shore, at the foot of the mountains, runs a path past a small village of light: a few homes with warm windows, a bench under a lamp post, a little bridge over a stream. Everyday life, drawn in light. The path then climbs in a gentle zigzag up the mountain toward the sky. Everything happens in the middle band of the screen, so the bottom third (the water's reflection) stays calm for the performers.
- **Lone lights:** seven small dim lights at the start, each a person who is alone: the elderly traveler on the path, others out on the dark water and in the mist. Every one of them is found by the end.
- **The peace ring:** the visual signature of this song. Whenever one of Mother's words is spoken, a slow ring of golden stillness spreads out from the speaker across the whole lake, the water turns to glass behind it, and the reflections of the stars sharpen. Where a mother's love reaches, peace settles.
## 5. Color, light and camera journey
- **Introduction:** the last amber of 06's evening cooling into twilight blue, a coral glow along the horizon, mist low on the water, warm village windows, only a few first stars. Camera at walking height, looking down the long path so it recedes deep into the distance, with a slow steady push forward.
- **Verse 1 (the greeting and the helping hand):** twilight blue with a coral horizon, warming to lantern gold where people meet; more first stars appear. Footprints of light glow along the path; the peace rings begin. Camera: a slow, respectful half circle around the two figures during the greeting (about 30 degrees, never closer than full length), then still while the hand is offered.
- **Verse 2 (the lost return):** silver-blue mist, cool violet, warming to amber as lights come home. A river of lanterns spreads across the water. Camera rises into a high, gliding aerial over the mist, then descends to the shore for the reunions.
- **Hush (2:17 to 2:27):** the dusk has deepened to a clear dark blue; the mist is gone and every star is doubled in a mirror lake. Camera stops completely.
- **Verse 3 (yearning for the kingdom):** pre-dawn rose gold along the ridge, then a heavenly dawn of pearl and gold. Clouds open on a vast city of light above the mountains. Camera tilts slowly from lake to sky, then cranes up beside the procession.
- **Climax and ending:** lantern gold pouring over a twilight blue lake; the arch glowing; the gate of the kingdom open. Camera wide, majestic and still for the hold.
**How the music drives it:** the mountain skyline breathes with the loudness, as in song 3. Note onsets send small rings across the water, softened in the quiet parts. The three big lifts (0:53, 1:51, 2:54) are the singer's promises, so they are where the warm light spreads widest: our answer is when the world gets warmer. The measured dips are breaths, used for Mother's wishes. The hush at 2:17 is total stillness, the turning point from searching to going home.
## 6. Mother's three wishes: a reverent treatment of their own
The three quoted lines are Heavenly Mother's own words, treated unlike anything else in the concert:
- **The quotation marks come first.** Two small glowing quotation marks appear high in the sky, far apart, like two candle flames, a breath before the line is sung. The words then appear between them. Only these three wishes use this.
- **Her lettering:** soft pearl white (about #FFF4E0) with a faint gold edge, in the concert's elegant serif but in italics, as if handwritten. Our words and the expressions stay upright.
- **The world listens:** while a wish is on screen the floating sparks slow almost to a stop, the water rings soften, and the horizon star glows brighter, its warmth reaching every lone light like a hand on the shoulder.
- **Only a short part of each wish appears as words** (section 8); the picture carries the rest.
- **Kept, not faded:** each wish rests about 6 to 8 seconds, then sinks gently into the horizon star, so Her words are kept there all through the song.
## 7. Section by section, line by line
### Opening and title (0:00 to 0:25)
The screen comes up from song 06's last frame (section 11) as 06's warm evening cools into blue dusk. On the lakeside path an elderly traveler of light walks slowly, bent a little under a heavy bundle. As the traveler passes, the trail of light behind becomes the song title, "May No One Ever Feel Lonely", large and centered in the middle band. When the band fills in at 0:13.5 (measured), the concert title "The Words of Mother's Love Healing Concert" appears beneath as the horizon star breathes once. After about 5 seconds both drift up and settle small in the top left. Two or three figures pass the traveler quickly in the other direction, busy, not unkind, just not noticing. The traveler sets the bundle down and sits on the bench under the lamp post, light dimming a little. Loneliness, told gently in one picture, the kind anyone in the hall has seen on an ordinary day.
### Verse 1: no one lonely on the path to heaven (about 0:26 to 1:18)
**Mother's wish (about 0:26):** the glowing quotation marks appear, then the fragment "on the path to heaven" (fragment 1). From the horizon star toward the bench, footprints of light appear one by one along the path, as if someone has already walked this way to find the lonely one. The traveler lifts their head as the light arrives. Mother's wish literally lights the way to the lonely one, and shows us where to walk.
**Earnest wish line (about 0:38):** no words. The wish sinks into the horizon star. One of the figures who walked past stops, turns back and looks: the moment of noticing. They begin to walk back toward the bench.
**The greeting (about 0:52 to 1:01, first big lift, nothing else on screen):** the helper reaches the bench, bows slightly, then kneels to be at the traveler's eye level, one hand resting gently on the bench beside them. Their light turns coral. **Greeting** "How are you?" rises softly between them and holds about 8 seconds (section 9). The traveler's light, dim until now, brightens from within, like a smile seen only as light. The first peace ring spreads from the bench across the whole lake.
**The breath (about 1:01 to 1:04):** no words; the lake turns to glass.
**The helping hand (about 1:04 to 1:13):** the helper notices the heavy bundle, lifts it onto their own shoulder, and offers their other hand. The traveler takes it and rises. **Consideration** "Can I give you a hand with anything?" appears above the joined hands and holds about 8 seconds. A second peace ring spreads. They walk on together, slowly, at the traveler's pace.
**United with brothers and sisters (about 1:13 to 1:19):** from the warm windows of the village, other figures step out and join them on the path, one greeting the next. A small family of light walks together along the shore. The first lone light is found.
### Verse 2: the lost sheep return (about 1:20 to 2:16)
**Mother's wish (about 1:20):** a pale mist rises over the lake. Out on the water, far apart, faint lights drift away from the shore: the lost ones, wandering. The quotation marks appear, and "please return" (fragment 2) is called across the water and echoes off the mountains.
**Earnest wish line (about 1:37):** no words. At the foot of the horizon star, on the shore, Heavenly Mother is seen from behind, robed in pearl-gold light, holding a lamp up toward the misty water, very still, waiting. The camera stays respectfully behind Her, Her lamp in the center of the frame.
**Mother's worried heart (about 1:51, second big lift; the moment for the male duet or quartet):** the villagers take lanterns from their windows and walk out onto the water's reflection into the mist. Among the first to go is the elderly traveler from verse 1, now walking upright, lantern held high: the one who was helped has become a helper. The camera rises into a high aerial: a river of warm lanterns spreading over cool silver mist, each one heading for a lost light. At the front walk two lantern-bearers side by side (four if a quartet sings), their lanterns swelling and softening with the men's voices: the singers on stage are the ones leading the search.
**The promise to console Her (about 2:04 to 2:16):** one by one the lost lights are reached. Each lantern-bearer offers a hand; the lost one takes it and they turn for home, the mist thinning behind them into clear, starry water. On the shore, families wait with open arms; each return is a joyful, dignified embrace of light, and with every embrace Mother's lamp grows brighter: Her joy shown only as light.
### The hush (2:17 to 2:27, measured)
The quietest moment after the opening. The mist is gone. The lake is a perfect mirror; every star is doubled. Everyone gathered stands still on the shore around Mother's glowing lamp, shoulder to shoulder. Nothing moves, nothing is written. Peace, completely settled.
### Verse 3: yearning for the eternal kingdom (about 2:28 to 3:20)
**Mother's wish (about 2:28):** high above the mountains the clouds part like curtains, and the kingdom of heaven appears: a vast city of light rising in terraces, its gates shining, softly radiant, far above everything. The quotation marks appear and "the eternal kingdom" (fragment 3) is written in the rays pouring down through the opening.
**Earnest wish line (about 2:41):** no words. The rose gold of a heavenly dawn spreads along the ridge. Every head of light on the shore turns upward; children of light are lifted onto shoulders to see.
**The promise to share love (about 2:54, the long lift begins):** everyone joins hands along the shore and the light runs through the joined hands like a current, until the whole line glows as one river of light. Then, along the far shores of the lake and beyond the mountains, other villages light up in answer, and their lights begin walking toward the path: the warm words spreading across the world.
**The promise to go home (about 3:07 to 3:20):** the path's reflection in the water lifts into the sky and becomes a bridge of light from the shore to the open gate. Heavenly Mother, from behind, steps onto it first, Her lamp held high and Her robe trailing light. All the lights follow Her in a procession, the elderly traveler and the helper walking side by side, hand in hand. The camera cranes up with them.
### Climax (about 3:21 to 3:44, measured loudest at 3:29 to 3:43)
The procession, now thousands strong, crosses the bridge into the sky. "my heavenly home" (fragment 4) forms out of the procession itself. At the peak of the music the gate opens fully and a flood of warm gold pours down over the mountains and across the lake: the whole world glows. Every lone light from the opening is in the line. No one is alone.
### Ending (3:44 to 4:04) and the hold after the music
The procession passes into the gate with Mother. The last to enter are the traveler and the helper, who pause together at the gate for a heartbeat and look back at the lake, now shining and calm. The gate softens to a warm, breathing glow, and the sky around it settles back into deep blue night full of stars, ready for song 08. Below, a final peace ring spreads across the lake. The arch of nine lights shines above with this song's stars glowing a little warmer. This frame is held still after the music ends (from about 4:04), with only slow particle drift and the gentle breathing of the gate, for at least 90 seconds, so a longer live performance never goes black. The bottom third stays calm throughout.
## 8. Lyric fragments on screen (five, short, each with its own effect)
Revision 2 removes the earlier "with all of my heart" fragment so that nothing competes with the two expressions.
1. **"on the path to heaven"** (Mother's wish, verse 1, about 0:26). **Effect: footprints of light.** Inside the glowing quotation marks, each word appears exactly as a glowing footprint lands on the path below, step by step from the horizon star toward the lonely one. **Why:** Her wish is that no one walks the road home in the dark or alone; Her words walk the path first and show us the way.
2. **"please return"** (Mother's wish, verse 2, about 1:20). **Effect: an echo across the water.** The words appear in pearl white, then echo off the mountains: two softer copies drift away across the lake, each fainter. With each echo, one lost light in the mist stops drifting and turns toward home. **Why:** a mother calling for children who have wandered; Her call carries across the whole lake and reaches them.
3. **"the eternal kingdom"** (Mother's wish, verse 3, about 2:28). **Effect: written in heavenly rays.** The letters are built from the rays pouring through the parted clouds, as if the kingdom writes its own name; the rays shimmer and the words stay high above the mountains. **Why:** the line is a yearning for a place above us; the words come from above, not from us.
4. **"my heavenly home"** (singer, climax, about 3:21 to 3:44). **Effect: the procession becomes the words.** The line of thousands of walking lights curls across the bridge so that, for a few seconds, its bends form the words in gold, then straightens and walks on into the gate. **Why:** home is all of us going there together; every light found during the song is part of these letters.
5. **"our Mother's earnest wish"** (the refrain after each wish, verse 1, about 0:38; added in revision 3 and used in the promo). **Effect: answered from the windows.** One by one the warm windows of the village brighten, and from each a small light drifts up to become one letter, so the village itself writes the words in soft gold. **Why:** the refrain is the children taking Mother's wish to heart; the homes along the path answer Her together.
Optional sixth, only if Anthony wants one: "the boundless love" (verse 3, about 2:54), drawn by the current of light through joined hands.
## 9. Expressions and the arch
Each expression has its own uncrowded moment of about 8 to 10 seconds: no lyric fragment, no other text and no other movement competes. Shared style: small spaced capital label above the phrase, glowing warm-white serif tinted in its color, large enough to read from the back. Each is followed by a peace ring and a short breath, so the audience sees the word, then sees the peace it brings.
- **GREETING "How are you?"** coral #FF977C. **Tied to:** Mother's wish that no one be lonely on the path to heaven, shown at its answer, the start of the singer's promise. **Time:** about 0:52 to 1:01 (locked to the measured first big lift at 0:53). **Interaction:** the helper turns back, bows slightly and kneels to eye level beside the traveler on the bench; the traveler's light brightens. **Effect:** coral light gathers between them and the words rise from it; after they rest, their sparks fly up into the arch and the first peace ring spreads. **Star:** 1 (Greeting).
- **CONSIDERATION "Can I give you a hand with anything?"** rose #EF7A96. **Tied to:** the promise to care for brothers and sisters (verse 1, last line). **Time:** about 1:04 to 1:13 (estimate). **Interaction:** the helper lifts the heavy bundle onto their own shoulder and offers a hand; the traveler takes it and rises; they walk on together at the traveler's pace. **Effect:** rose light runs along the outstretched arm, the words appear above the joined hands, then collapse into a rose star that rises into the arch, followed by the second peace ring. **Star:** 8 (Consideration).
**Why these:** the campaign calls Greeting the first phrase to peace, a heartfelt hello to the people we pass every day, and the simplest answer to someone alone on the road is exactly that hello. I placed it a few seconds after Mother's wish, at the moment we answer it, so Her own words stay untouched by any overlay. Consideration fits the promise to care for others, and the campaign's own first example (offering a hand to an older person carrying something heavy) is the scene shown. Verses 2 and 3 are beautiful but are not exactly any of the nine acts, so no expression is forced there.
**The arch (star history, DESIGN-SYSTEM.md 12a):** when song 07 begins, stars 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9 are lit (lit by songs 01, 03, 04, 05 and 06); star 1 and star 2 are dark. Song 07 lights star 1 (Greeting) for the first time in the concert: the coral words collapse into a new light at the left end of the arch, and the line from star 1 to star 2 does not grow yet because star 2 is still dark. Song 07 relights star 8 (Consideration, first lit in song 05): the rose words fly into it, and it gives a brief flare and gains one new thin ring. When song 07 ends, stars 1 and 3 to 9 are lit; only star 2 (Gratitude) waits for song 08.

The opening card rests top left. An elderly traveler of light, stooped under a heavy bundle, walks slowly along the path and sits down on the bench (special figure, two poses). From about 5 s Mother's wish "on the path to heaven" is written stroke by stroke in pearl italics (fragment style "write"), while lanterns rise. Transition at 13 s: a bloom of light from the horizon star.
- **Scene 2, Mother's wish lights the way (13 to 27 s):** plate "the lit path": the horizon star close and warm, a line of soft lights running along the shore from it toward the bench. "our Mother's earnest wish" forms in sparks. The lone light is found: one figure far along the path stops, turns back toward the bench and starts walking (shared library walking pose). Signature effect 1, the lone light found: a beam of light (BEAM) runs from the horizon star along the path to the traveler. Transition at 27 s: slow camera push through into the closer plate.
- **Scene 3, "How are you?" (27 to 40 s):** plate "the bench by the shore": close view of the bench under the lamp post, coral dusk. The helper reaches the bench, bows slightly, then kneels to the traveler's eye level (shared Greeting poses: approach, bow, kneel beside). The full words GREETING "How are you?" appear as the helper kneels (about 30 to 37 s), held about 8 seconds with nothing else written, then rise and light star 1 for the first time in the concert. The traveler's light brightens; a ring of stillness spreads across the water (the peace ring). Transition at 40 s: a ripple wipe across the lake.
- **Scene 4, The helping hand and the lantern procession (40 to 51 s):** plate "village path": the path climbing gently past warm village windows. The helper lifts the heavy bundle onto their own shoulder and offers a hand; the traveler takes it and rises (shared Consideration poses: notice, offer the hand, help up). The full words CONSIDERATION "Can I give you a hand with anything?" appear above the joined hands (about 41 to 49 s), then fly into star 8, which flares with one new ring. Signature effect 2, the lantern procession: villagers step out carrying lanterns and walk the path together; at the front two lantern-bearers walk side by side, their lanterns swelling with the voices (the male duet or quartet moment).
- **Scene 5, Toward home (51 to 60 s):** plate "gate of home": the path winding up toward a soft gate of light glowing in the clouds above the mountains, first stars all out. Heavenly Mother, seen only from behind and fully robed, holding a lamp high, leads the procession up the path (special figure, one pose). Signature effect 3, the gate of light: a gentle beam falls from the gate onto the path. The end card "The Words of Mother's Love" with the date, time and address forms above, and the scene holds calm to the end.
- **People of light:** the traveler (special: walking with bundle, seated on the bench), Heavenly Mother from behind with a raised lamp (special), helper and villagers from the shared library (walking, Greeting and Consideration poses, lantern-bearers). All clothed, faceless, small in the frame in the middle band; the bottom third stays calm water.
