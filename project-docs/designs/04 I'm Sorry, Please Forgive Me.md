# 04 I'm Sorry, Please Forgive Me: Video Design
**Design title:** "The Thaw: a heart melted by Mother's love"
Songbook 273. Recording: 04 I'm Sorry, Please Forgive Me.mp3 (studio, 3:41). Concert: The Words of Mother's Love That Bring Peace, LV Zion Healing Concert, Sunday December 6, 2026, 4:30 PM.
**Status:** revision 3 (2026-11-13), the continuity pass. It follows the concert-wide decisions adopted while Anthony is away (DESIGN-SYSTEM.md 12a), the new look for this song (a frozen pale winter morning, DESIGN-SYSTEM.md section 6), the star history, the live length and the hand-offs agreed by message with songs 03 and 05. Planning only for the full video; a 1 minute promo has been made with the engine's current effects (see "The promo").

## The message of this song
The Words of Mother's Love bring peace. This song carries two of them, practiced between two sisters: Apology, "I'm sorry. It must have been hard on you." and Inclusiveness, "It's okay. I understand." Everything in the video serves one thing: the audience should leave knowing these two sentences, picturing when to say them at home, at work or at school, and feeling in their hearts that saying them melts what is frozen between people and opens the way to the kingdom of heaven.

## What the campaign teaches this song
Studied 2026-11-13 from heavenlymotherslove.org (About, Daily Check, videos, peacemaker, reviews, news, second anniversary page) and Anthony's campaign app (home and the nine expressions).
- **The campaign's own picture of Apology is melting.** It calls Apology a phrase that melts the heart, by understanding the other person's feelings first. That confirmed the whole design: the hurt is ice, and the apology is the warmth that melts it. The thaw is no longer just a pretty idea; it is the campaign's own image made visible.
- **Apology means looking from the other side and going first.** The campaign asks us to reflect on our words and actions from the other person's point of view, apologize for our own mistakes, and extend our hand first. Its everyday example: think about how your words affected someone, admit the mistake, and take the first step toward making up. So in the video the first sister must clearly (1) see her sister's hidden hurt, (2) walk across first, and (3) offer her hand.
- **Peace comes with a humble heart.** The campaign ties Apology to humility. The song says the same twice ("truly humbled"). So the turning point of each verse is a bow, and the light of Mother's love reaches the sisters only when one bows low.
- **Inclusiveness is forgiving and embracing, without embarrassment.** The campaign describes it as a phrase of forgiveness that embraces faults: anyone can make mistakes, so embrace people kindly and generously, and when someone errs, respond gently and help them move forward without embarrassment. That gave the design its most important new interaction: when the first sister kneels, the hurt sister does not leave her down there. She kneels too, then gently lifts her sister to her feet and embraces her. Forgiveness that lets the other keep her dignity.
- **Mother's love is the source.** The campaign begins from a mother's first love: unconditional support, consideration, sacrifice and service, a love that crosses every nation and culture. Its saying is that where a mother's love reaches, peace settles. In the video, the warmth that melts the ice comes from Heavenly Mother (shown only as the low warm sun and the golden breath of light it sends) and passes through the sisters to each other.
- **These are small everyday words.** The campaign is about daily practice (the Daily Check asks each day which of the nine you said) at the family table, in an elevator, in the office. Its short films are exactly those scenes. So this song shows the hurt and the healing as ordinary moments: a family meal gone quiet, a door closed between two rooms, then the same table full and warm. The audience should recognize their own homes.
- **The fruit is real.** The campaign reports couples and families who restored relationships by saying "I'm sorry" in daily life. The ending shows that fruit spreading: one reconciliation becomes many, and many become one family of light.

## The idea
The video begins on a frozen pale winter morning: the home lake under ice, a low cold sun in haze, with a thin crack of cold blue light running across the ice between two shores. Inside the ice, faint like old photographs, are frozen everyday moments: a family table where two places sit apart, a door closed between two rooms, two cups of tea gone cold. A sister made of warm apricot light stands on one shore. On the other stands a sister made of cool silver light, with a small shard of ice glowing in her heart that can be seen only in the reflection. When the first sister finally sees it and says she is sorry, her warmth begins to melt the ice. She crosses first, offers her hand, and bows low. Then the low sun, the concert's sign of Heavenly Mother's love, sends a golden breath of warm light rolling across the ice, the way a mother breathes warmth into a child's cold hands; it gathers around the two sisters, and the whole lake begins to thaw. In the second verse we cross to the other shore and see the same moment from the hurt sister's side, and we watch her forgive: she lifts her sister up and embraces her. The frozen everyday moments thaw into the same moments made warm. By the end the lake is living water, many brothers and sisters have joined hands in a great circle, and in the mirror of the water beneath them shines the radiant gate of the kingdom of heaven. One long thaw: frost to living water, ice blue and pearl to apricot, rose and gold, a cold morning to a warm and heavenly one.

## Why this fits
- **Ice is the hidden pain.** The lyric confesses being blind to a pain held inside. Ice is hard, cold and clear, yet unseen until light shines through it. It also suits a December concert.
- **Warmth is humility and Mother's love.** Nothing in the video melts by force. Warmth spreads only when someone bows lower, and it is strongest when the golden breath of Mother's love surrounds them.
- **Seeing from the other side.** Verse 2 moves the camera across the lake to the hurt sister's shore, which is exactly what the campaign asks of an apology.
- **Everyday life.** The frozen and then thawed family scenes tie the heavenly picture back to the audience's own homes, workplaces and schools.
- **Hope for the kingdom.** The song looks toward the kingdom of heaven in every chorus. The video shows that hope growing: a doorway of light on the horizon in chorus 1, and the gate of the heavenly kingdom shining in the water at the end.
- **One world, its own look.** It starts from the shared lake and returns to it. Its look belongs to this song alone: the only frozen morning in the concert, ice blue, pearl and apricot, with a high horizon so the ice fills the screen (song 02 is a snowy night; 05 a spring afternoon).

## The people of light
- **The first sister (who apologizes):** formed of warm apricot and gold sparks (#FFAD69 at her core) with a glassy inner glow. Modest everyday clothes drawn in fine lines of light: a long-sleeved blouse, an ankle-length skirt, a soft knitted cardigan whose folds ripple as she moves. Hair gathered at the back as a simple glowing shape. Head a smooth glowing oval with no features.
- **The second sister (who was hurt):** cool silver-blue light (#AFC8E8), equally modest: a long winter coat over a dress, collar and buttons drawn in light, a scarf. Easy to tell apart from the back of the hall. In her heart, a small sharp shard of ice glows pale blue, barely visible in the air, clear in the reflection. As she forgives, her light warms from silver to rose (#F582AF).
- **How they move:** with dignity and tenderness, never theatrical. Every gesture is slow enough to read from the back of the hall: a hesitation before a step, a hand offered palm up and held there patiently, a head bowed, two foreheads almost touching, a hand resting on a shoulder. Their outlines breathe gently with the music. A soft trail of sparks follows each movement.
- **Scale:** about one fifth of the screen height in wide shots, up to one third in the close moments, always within the middle band. The bottom third stays calm ice or calm water.
- **Heavenly Mother:** never a figure and never a face in this song. Her love is the low sun on the horizon and the golden breath of warm light it sends across the ice. Father is shown only as light: a golden pillar beside the sun. (Revision 2 used vast robed hands of light; songs 03, 05 and 06 also use Mother's hands, so this song now uses the breath of light instead, to keep every song's effects its own.)
- **The many:** in the last part, dozens and then hundreds of small figures of light in everyday clothes: families with children, an older couple, coworkers, classmates. All faceless, all modest, all joyful in their posture.

## The video, section by section

### Hand-off from song 03 (before the first note)
Agreed by message with song 03. Song 04 opens on song 03's closing hold exactly: a soft morning lake, mirror still (sky #13265c, #7d5086, #f1a67c at the horizon), the sun just above the mountains at the left, a pale gold-seamed moon upper middle, rose petals on the water, "I love You Mother" a faint reflection, and the arch with stars 3, 7 and 9 lit. During 04's instrumental introduction the faint words fade, the colors drain to ice blue and pearl, the sun settles low and cold behind haze, and frost spreads from both shores, freezing the petals under clear ice. Even after great love, small hurts can quietly freeze between people. The petals are the one carried object; the thaw frees them.

### Introduction and title, 0:00 to 0:31 (measured quiet section)
- **Scene:** the home lake frozen into pale pearl ice under a high horizon, so the ice fills more of the screen; the mountains and their reflection blurred and cold. A pale winter morning sky, ice blue at the top fading to pearl at the horizon, with a thin haze. A low sun sits just above the mountains, small and cold behind the haze. Tiny ice crystals glitter in the air, catching the light.
- **The crack:** a thin line of cold blue light runs across the ice from shore to shore, just below the horizon, pulsing faintly like a held breath.
- **The frozen moments:** as the camera drifts low over the ice, we glimpse beneath its surface, faint as old photographs drawn in frost lines: a family table with two place settings far apart, a door closed between two rooms, two cups of tea gone cold side by side. No people in them, only the traces of a hurt in ordinary life. They are dim and small, so the mood is quiet, not sad.
- **Title (0:03 to 0:11):** "I'm Sorry, Please Forgive Me" appears melted into the ice: a soft apricot warmth passes beneath the frozen surface from left to right, and where it passes, the letters thaw into clear, glowing water lines in the pearl ice. They lift off as warm sparks and settle large in the upper third, with "The Words of Mother's Love Healing Concert" beneath in a smaller warm-white serif. Hold about 5 seconds. (Revision 2 grew the title as frost; song 02 also uses a frost-written title, so this song melts its title instead, which is the campaign's own image of Apology.)
- **0:11 to 0:16:** both titles drift to the top left and rest small.
- **0:16 to 0:31:** the two sisters gather out of sparks, one on each shore: the warm one on the left, the cool one on the right, the crack between them. Their backs are half turned from each other. The camera drifts slowly toward the left sister and settles behind her shoulder, so we share her view. Each piano note leaves a delicate ring of frost light on the ice.

### Line 1.1, about 0:31 to 0:52: she sees, and says she is sorry
- **0:31 to 0:39, she sees:** the left sister turns toward her sister and, for the first time, looks down at the ice between them. In the reflection beneath her sister, we see the ice shard glowing in her heart. Fragment 1 appears here (see Lyric fragments). She lays a hand on her own heart. She had not seen it before; now she does.
- **0:41 to 0:51, Apology expression (10 seconds, nothing else on screen moves except the snow):** see Expressions.
- **Color:** the first warmth enters the frame, only around the left sister: a soft apricot glow at her feet, the ice there turning clear and lit from within.

### Line 1.2, about 0:52 to 1:04: she goes first, offers her hand, bows
- **Going first:** she hesitates a moment at the edge of the ice (it takes courage to go first), then steps out. Each footstep melts a small round pool of warm water that glows apricot and stays behind her like stepping stones. The path of melted footprints is the picture of reaching out first.
- **The hand offered:** she stops a respectful step away from her sister, who still does not turn. She holds out her hand, palm up, and waits. The waiting is part of the apology. After a long breath, the second sister's hand, cold silver, slowly comes to rest in hers. Where the two hands touch, a small warm light blooms.
- **The bow (Fragment 2):** still holding the hand, the first sister lowers herself to one knee on the ice and bows her head.
- **Light:** the moment she kneels, the horizon star brightens for the first time and a low warm beam travels across the ice to her bowed shoulders. Bowing low lets the light reach her.

### Line 1.3, about 1:04 to 1:15 (measured build): her heart opens
- The warm beam passes through their joined hands into the second sister. The ice shard in her heart softens and turns from blue to rose.
- **Fragment 3:** the shard unfolds into a small flower of light, and the second sister turns at last to face her kneeling sister. She places her free hand gently on her sister's bowed head, like a blessing.
- As the music builds, the crack in the ice fills with warm gold from the center outward, like gold seams in mended pottery, so the line that divided them becomes the most beautiful line on the lake.

### Chorus 1, 1:15 to 1:42 (measured big section): the breath of Mother's love and the first thaw
- **1:15 to 1:21 (calling on Father and Mother):** the two sisters, now side by side, turn their backs to us and face the low sun. Beside it rises a tall golden pillar of light (Father) and the sun itself swells into soft white-gold radiance (Mother). The haze begins to glow.
- **1:21 to 1:29 (the warm heart, measured loudest at 1:24):** from the radiance on the horizon a golden breath of warm light rolls across the ice toward the sisters, low and soft like warm mist, the way a mother breathes into a child's cold hands. Where it passes, frost turns to beads of light and the ice clears. It gathers around the two small sisters as a glowing hearth-warm cocoon. The ice beneath them melts into a wide circle of open, glowing water, and the warmth spreads outward. This is the heart of the song visually: the warm heart that lets her love her sister is given by Mother. Inside the warmth, the sisters turn to each other and hold both hands.
- **1:29 to 1:35 (the Kingdom of Heaven):** the warm cocoon rises gently like breath in cold air and thins into the sky, which brightens from pearl to pale apricot as the haze lifts and the first warm rays break through. The melted footprint stones join into a glowing path across the water toward the horizon, where the golden pillar and the radiance lean together into a high doorway of light, softly shining, its far side too bright to see into. The sisters take their first steps on the path, hand in hand. Hope, not yet arrival.
- **1:35 to 1:42 (never change):** the path does not fade. It sets from liquid apricot into a steady gold line that stays for the rest of the song: a promise that does not change.
- **Water, sky and the frozen moments:** open living water in the center with the mountains reflected clearly again; ice remains near both shores. Under the thawing ice, the first frozen moment warms: the two cold cups of tea now steam gently, side by side.

### Interlude, 1:42 to 1:48 (measured drop): crossing to the other side
- The camera rises slightly and glides across the lake past the sisters to the right shore. The shores trade places; we now stand behind the second sister. The warmth sinks back to the pale cold morning of her memory, because this is the same story told again from her side.
- For a few seconds we see her memory, faint: the frozen lake, her sister far away, and herself alone holding the ice shard (seen in reflection). This is how it felt to be the one who was hurt.

### Line 2.1, about 1:48 to 2:09: she came to me first, and I forgave
- **1:48 to 1:55 (Fragment 4):** in the memory, the first sister walks toward us across the ice, the footprints now coming toward the hurt sister's side.
- **1:55 to 1:57:** she arrives and opens her arms. The second sister stands stiffly for one long moment, arms at her sides.
- **1:57 to 2:07, Inclusiveness expression (10 seconds, nothing competing):** the second sister's arms rise and she returns the embrace, then draws her sister close so her head rests on her shoulder: it's okay. See Expressions.
- **Color:** rose pink flows out of the second sister for the first time and mixes with apricot where they hold each other. Under the ice beside them, the closed door from the frozen moments swings open and warm light spills through it.

### Line 2.2, about 2:09 to 2:21: she held my hands, and I lifted her up
- The memory becomes the present. We now see the kneeling moment from the hurt sister's side: her sister kneeling, small and humble, lit from behind by the horizon star.
- The second sister does not leave her down there. She kneels too, so the two are level and no one is above the other. Then she rises and, with both hands, gently lifts her sister to her feet, the way the campaign describes it: helping someone move forward without embarrassment. They stand together, foreheads almost touching.
- No words here on purpose. The gesture is the message.

### Line 2.3, about 2:21 to 2:32 (build): Mother's love through her humbleness
- From the receiving side we see what the hurt sister felt: the warm beam from the horizon enters the first sister's bowed back, flows down her arms, through their joined hands, and blossoms in the second sister's heart. Mother's love, arriving through a humble person.
- The golden breath of light returns, high and soft over the lake this time, resting above it like a blessing. Along both shores the remaining ice begins to glow from beneath, ready to melt.

### Chorus 2, 2:32 to 2:59 (repeat of chorus 1, bigger): the full thaw, glorious
- **2:32 to 2:38:** the camera rises to a high centered view where both sisters are seen equally on the gold path in the middle of the lake. Neither shore is "ours" anymore.
- **2:38 to 2:46 (peak about 2:41):** all remaining ice melts at once in a great wave of light from the center outward. Plates of ice turn into floating lanterns of warm light as they melt and drift up into the sky by the hundreds. The haze lifts completely and the sky becomes a warm, clear winter morning: apricot and rose at the horizon, soft blue above, with long warm rays fanning from the low sun. The petals from song 03, released from the ice, rise and swirl. The sisters laugh in their posture: heads tilted together, one turning the other in a small joyful step, sharing one shawl of light.
- **The frozen moments made warm:** beneath the clear water now, the family table is full and glowing, every place set close together; the open door leads into a lit room. Everyday life, healed.
- **2:46 to 2:52:** the doorway of light on the horizon opens wider and its glow pours across the water toward them. The gold path runs all the way to it.
- **2:52 to 2:59 (proclaiming boundless love):** from the sisters, rings of warm light travel out across the whole lake and past the edges of the screen. Wherever a ring touches a shore, small figures of light gather out of sparks: a father and child, an older couple, coworkers, classmates, many more. Two who had stood apart on a shore turn and take each other's hands. The thaw is spreading from one heart to many.

### Final section, 2:59 to 3:36: one family of light, and a glimpse of the kingdom
- **2:59 to 3:15:** the many walk out onto the water along new paths of light that branch from the sisters' gold path, greeting one another as they meet, until a wide ring of people holding hands surrounds the two sisters. From slightly above, the ring and its reflection form one perfect circle.
- **3:06 to 3:17 (measured peaks), the kingdom in the water:** as the circle closes, the reflection beneath it changes. The water no longer mirrors the mountains: it reveals, shining up from below, the gate of a heavenly city of light, pearl-white and gold, its walls glowing, with the circle of people reflected standing inside its open gate. Above the water, everyday people holding hands; below, the same people in the kingdom of heaven. The peace they practice here is the beginning of heaven.
- **3:15 to 3:25:** the circle glows brightest. If verse 3's chorus is sung, Fragment 5 appears here; otherwise the circle says it without words.
- **3:25 to 3:36 (closing phrase):** the figures dissolve into sparks from the outer ring inward, rising in a slow golden spiral toward the arch of nine. The two sisters are last. Their joined hands are the final shape to dissolve, leaving one small warm light resting on the water where they stood.
- **3:36 to 3:41.5:** silence. The lake is open and mirror still; the gold path remains; the heavenly city fades softly back into the reflection of the warm morning sky.

### Ending hold after the music (about 80 seconds)
- A calm, warm late morning (agreed by message with song 05 as the 04 to 05 frame): the thawed lake mirror still in apricot light, the low sun bright on the horizon, the arch above with stars 3, 4, 7 and 9 lit, a thin gold path across the water from center to the horizon, and one small warm light resting on the water at center where the sisters' joined hands dissolved, sending a gentle ripple every few seconds. Then the closing tagline: "Where a mother's love reaches, peace settles."

## Lyric fragments on screen (four, plus one if verse 3 is sung)
Each is drawn in light in an elegant thin serif with a faint glow, in the upper or middle third, never in the bottom third, and never at the same time as an expression. Each effect is unique to this song.

### Fragment 1: "the pain you held inside" (about 0:32 to 0:39)
**Words that exist only in the reflection.** Look at the air and there is nothing. Look at the reflection in the ice below the second sister, and there are the words in pale blue frost, inside the reflected shard in her heart, spreading from it like frost on glass. The camera tilts gently toward the ice so the audience discovers the words exactly where the first sister does. The words never rise into the air: instead the ice above them slowly clears from the edges inward, as if warmed by her look, until the words lie plainly visible in clear water, then fade. **Why:** the pain was real but hidden; you see it only by looking from another angle, from the other side, and seeing it is what begins the thaw. (Other songs raise words out of the reflection; this one keeps them in it and melts the ice instead.)

### Fragment 2: "truly humbled" (about 0:58 to 1:04)
**Words that are whole only when they bow.** The letters stand upright above the ice line, but only their top halves exist. As the first sister kneels, the letters bend forward one by one until their bases touch the ice and meet their own reflections. Only then, joined to their reflections, do they become whole and glow apricot. **Why:** humility completes us by going lower, and the campaign says peace comes with a humble heart.

### Fragment 3: "opened her heart" (about 1:08 to 1:15)
**Words that bloom.** The ice shard in the second sister's heart becomes a bud of light that rises before her and opens petal by petal; each petal carries part of the words in thin rose-gold script, so the phrase can be read only when the flower is fully open. The flower floats down onto the ice and stays as a small lotus of light until the thaw. **Why:** a closed heart opening is the turning point of verse 1, and the flower grows from the very thing that was frozen.

### Fragment 4: "came to me first" (about 1:48 to 1:55)
**Words written in footsteps.** As the first sister walks toward us, each melted footprint glows with a letter inside it, so the phrase is written by her walk, read in the order she walks it. When she arrives, the letters lift together for a moment in the middle of the screen, then settle back into stepping stones. **Why:** going first is the gift the hurt sister thanks her for, and the campaign asks us to extend our hand first. Her steps literally write it.
