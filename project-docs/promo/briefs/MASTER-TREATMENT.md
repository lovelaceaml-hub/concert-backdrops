# The masterpiece: "The Words That Bring Peace" (working title)

Anthony's bar: majestic, beautiful, awesome, brilliant, extravagant, beyond imagination, heavenly, very expressive; so breathtakingly beautiful that emotions are felt. Engine only. One film, the best of all five, built around the theme: the words of Mother's love that bring peace.

## The idea
Heavenly Mother's words of love fall from heaven as light. Wherever a word lands, light, colour and peace bloom: in people, in nature, across the whole world, until the Earth itself glows and settles between two hearts as the logo.
Heavenly Mother appears only as hands of light (never a face): her open hands release the first word at the start, and cradle the glowing world at the end.

## The look
Heavenly and grand: an endless sea of golden clouds lit from within, cathedral shafts of light, aurora curtains, rivers of light, prismatic sparkle, blossoming light, hearts. Never dark, cold or empty. People: few, whole and radiant, in two looks only (crystal light and warm gold glitter), in tender acted moments. Camera: one continuous, soaring journey (descend, glide, rise) with sweeping moves on the beat.
