# Ten films: ten different concert promos, one director each

Anthony asked: "Make 10 different videos. Direct fresh and new ideas for each one. Parallel work." Each is a different 60 second promo for the Healing Concert "The Words of Mother's Love", built on the new 3D layer (see P3D-BRIEF.md in this folder, with the reference photos and his words). He will compare them, so each must be its own idea: its own world, metaphor, camera language, palette and way of showing the words.

## What every film must do
- 60.0 s on the new high-energy medley (audio/promo/concert-medley.mp3 from branch epic-medley2 when it lands; 7 or 8 song sections of about 7 to 8 s, energy high from the first second, one short breath about two thirds through, the biggest passage last). Until its section map exists, plan with sections starting at about 0, 7.5, 15, 22.5, 30, 37.5, 45, 52.5 s.
- Excite people. The first second must grab (never open on black or on a slow fade). Something new happens at every section start. The camera is always moving, and every move is motivated.
- Show all nine campaign expressions (the Words of Mother's Love), with their exact campaign wording and labels, as words made of light living in the world (helper WORDS, branch engine-p3d-words). They may overlap and transform into each other or into people; each needs one clear reading moment of about 2.5 s or more, sharp and legible on a phone.
- People are lifelike human forms made of particles (helper BODY, branch engine-p3d-bodies), in the spirit of the reference photos: they gather out of light and scatter back into it. Interpretive, never a literal cartoon figure. Ordinary people may appear in featureless side profile. Heavenly Mother only from behind (veiled) or as hands. Everyone clothed in garments of light. No facial features anywhere.
- End on the concert: the title "The Words of Mother's Love", "Healing Concert", "Sunday, December 6 · 4:30 PM", "World Mission Society Church of God", "6720 W Sahara Ave, Las Vegas", readable for at least 4 s, in the existing end card style (the end card rework is on hold; do not redesign it). The nine lights of the arch may appear but are not required.
- No lyrics on screen. No em or en dash anywhere. Nothing dark behind text.
- Lessons from the first film's review (read A = scratchpad/epic/attack/REPORT.md): do not shoot it as one distant wide shot of tiny figures; go close; give the emotional beat real contact; make the loudest moment visible with colour and motion; never fade to black at the end.

## The ten ideas (each director may deepen and improve its own, but must stay clearly different from the others)

### 01 The Wave
One word becomes a wave of light. Darkness and a still black sea; a single spark drops and a ring of particles races outward. The camera rides the wavefront in one continuous forward flight, low over the water, for the whole film. Everything the wave touches wakes: people gather out of dust as it passes them, each expression is carried on the crest and left standing behind it like a wake, hills light up, and at the end the wave reaches the horizon and breaks as sunrise. Camera: one unbroken forward surf, banking and rising with the music. Palette: black sea to gold dawn.

### 02 Hands
The whole film is told with hands of light, in close-up (refs 04 and 14). Two hands of different colours reach and touch; a palm blows a stream of sparks; hands pass a small flame; hands cup water that glows; a hand lifts another; many hands rise together; at the end Heavenly Mother's two open hands hold the light of the whole world. Every expression is born from what a pair of hands releases. Camera: intimate macro orbits with very shallow focus and huge bokeh, snapping wider on the big beats. Palette: gold and blue meeting, warming to white.

### 03 Constellations
People are constellations. In a deep night sky, bodies made of points joined by fine lines (refs 03 and 12) hang among the stars; as a word passes from one to another a line of light joins them, and a web of connection grows across the whole dome. The camera flies through the star field like a planetarium ride and revolves around the growing web, which closes into the arch of nine lights and then into a turning globe with lights spreading across it. Each expression is a constellation that draws itself. Palette: indigo, silver, electric blue, a gold dawn rim at the end.

### 04 Windows
Double exposure (refs 08 and 02). Huge featureless profiles and figures stand as windows: inside each silhouette is another sky, different for each song section (clouds at sunrise, a sea, a field of stars, falling blossom, rain turning to light). Birds fly out of the silhouettes carrying the words. The camera pushes through a silhouette into the world inside it, where the next silhouette stands, again and again: one endless push-in. Palette: soft daylight pastels, pearl and peach, with deep skies inside the windows.

### 05 Petals
A garden of words on the wind (ref 07). Every expression is a blossom: petals spiral off people of light, cross a bright lake meadow, and land as letters; people form out of drifting petals and let them go again. The camera is itself a petal on the wind: it glides low, tumbles, lifts on the gusts that hit with the beats, and finally rises over a whole tree of light in bloom. Palette: the campaign day colours, cream, sky blue, coral, fresh green, with gold light.

### 06 Ascension
Everything rises (refs 05, 11, 13). One continuous vertical move for sixty seconds: from the surface of a dark lake up through mist, through shafts of light where people stand and dissolve upward into flakes, through the clouds, to the arch of lights above the clouds at sunrise. The flakes that leave each person become the words, which rise with the camera and become stars. Camera: an unbroken crane up that turns slowly as it climbs. Palette: deep blue below, gold flakes, white and rose above the clouds.

### 07 The Relay
One light carried by many. A runner made of points (refs 12 and 15) sprints across the frame holding a flame, sheds light as she goes, and hands the flame to the next person at each song section; with every hand-over an expression bursts from the touch and the world behind changes (night road, rain of sparks, dawn shore, field of lights, sea, a hill of lanterns). The camera tracks alongside at speed with motion blur and whips around at each hand-over. The last runner brings the flame to Heavenly Mother's open hands, seen from behind, and the day breaks from them. Palette: gold flame against changing cool worlds, to full daylight.

### 08 Storm to Peace
The words bring peace, shown literally. It opens in a storm: a heaving sea of particles, rain of sparks, wind, people of light apart and braced against it. Each expression lands like a drop of calm: where a word falls the sea goes still as glass and the people there turn toward each other. Calm spreads section by section until the whole sea is a mirror at sunrise and everyone stands together. Camera: fast aerial sweeps through rain and spray at first, settling into long glides as the peace spreads. Palette: storm slate and violet lightning to pearl, gold and sky blue.

### 09 The Veil
Following Heavenly Mother from behind for the whole film (refs 10, 13, 16 for light). Her veil is a river of particles that streams out behind and around her across the world; wherever the veil passes, people gather out of its light and the words appear in its folds. The camera flies along the veil like a ribbon road, weaves through it, and revolves behind her as rays break around her head; at the end the veil rises and becomes the sky and the arch. She is never seen from the front. Palette: warm white and gold on deep blue, rose at the end.

### 10 Nine Colours
Each expression has its own colour. Nine ribbons of coloured light race through the dark like comets; the camera chases them like a rollercoaster. Each ribbon writes its words in the air, wraps around a person who gathers out of its light and moves with it (a dancer, a walker, a child lifted, two people meeting), and races on. At the end the nine ribbons braid together into one white light that becomes the dawn and the arch of nine lights, each light keeping its colour. Palette: nine clear colours on black, resolving to white and daylight.
