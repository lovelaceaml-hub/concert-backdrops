# Film 01 The Wave: phase A (branch ten/01-the-wave, head 61b41f1, engine/songs/ten_01_the_wave.py)
Logline: one word falls into a sleeping black sea and becomes the edge of morning. In one unbroken shot we surf that wave of light as it wakes everyone it touches, grows with each of the nine words, and breaks on the far shore as the sunrise.
Timeline (no cuts; night ahead of the wave, dawn behind): 0 to 7.5 drop in: spark meets its mirror image (0.9), whip pan onto a hand-high ripple, macro at 30 cm, first sleeper wakes; 7.5 to 15 cutback: her profile on the edge of morning, look back, dolly zoom on two people, whip forward into a sea of sleepers; 15 to 22.5 stall: lantern field ignites, two who stood back to back, macro of hands touching (22.1); 22.5 to 30 barrel: inside the folded wave, time stops, we fly through the words; 30 to 37.5 trim: full revolve round two people walking the crest at 22 m/s, hills light up; 37.5 to 45 hang (the breath): a 27 m wall of light stops for one kneeling person, hand on shoulder, crane up; 45 to 52.5 big drop: 44 m/s, hands from the wave lift sleepers, hundreds on the crest, arms lifted, backs to us; 52.5 to 60 break: plume, sun through the arch, night swept off the sky, end card 55.6 to 60.
