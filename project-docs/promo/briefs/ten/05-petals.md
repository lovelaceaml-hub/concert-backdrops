# Film 05 Petals: phase A (branch ten/05-petals, head 76565a3, engine/songs/ten_05_petals.py)
Logline: a kind word is a petal: you cannot keep it, only open your hand. The wind takes it, and wherever it lands someone blossoms. We are one petal, blown from a palm in the first second, riding nine words round a flooded spring meadow, home to the tree every petal came from. Full daylight. The camera is the petal. The arch is a tree of nine boughs.
