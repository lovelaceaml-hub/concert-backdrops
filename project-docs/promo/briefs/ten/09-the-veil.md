# Film 09 The Veil: phase A (branch ten/09-the-veil, head 3b5a334, engine/songs/ten_09_the_veil.py)
Logline: we follow Heavenly Mother from behind across a night lake toward the first light. Her veil streams back as a river of light; wherever it reaches, people rise out of it and say the nine words to one another, until at sunrise she lifts her arms and the veil becomes the dawn sky and the arch. Every song change is a pass through cloth.
