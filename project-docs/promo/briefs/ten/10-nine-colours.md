# Film 10 Nine Colours: phase A (branch ten/10-nine-colours, head d2d4d19, engine/songs/ten_10_nine_colours.py)
Logline: Mother's love is one white light and her nine words are its colours. The white breaks into nine ribbons; we ride them like a rollercoaster as each writes its words and makes people of its own light; every touch of two colours flares white; all nine braid back into white: the morning. Colours from DESIGN-SYSTEM section 5.
Timeline: 0 to 1.2 over Mother's cupped hands (hands only) a white seed bursts into nine comets; to 7.5 LAUNCH coral, low over a black mirror lake, a walker lifts a hand; to 15 THE GATE blue, tunnel of 16 half hoops the mirror completes; to 22.5 THE EMBRACE apricot and pink, descending helix, hands touch, first white flare; to 30 THE HALF LOOP lavender, a dancer leads her ribbon, we ride upside down; to 37.5 THE DROP rose and gold, 34 m dive, a lifted child blows gold, lanterns wake; to 45 THE BREATH clay, two profiles on gold bokeh; to 52.5 THE BRAID violet, plunge to one who prays, comets converge, corkscrew up to white; to 60 MORNING, white becomes the sun, the column lands as the arch of nine coloured lights, end card 55 to 60.
Expressions: Greeting, Concession, Apology, Inclusiveness, Compliment, Consideration, Gratitude, Respect, Encouragement (each read 2.6 to 3.4 s).
Wow: the prism opening; the upside down half loop (the lake is the sky); the braid to white to arch.
Engine needs: (1) camera up vector or quaternion (CameraState.basis() flips at vertical); (2) ribbons: continuous tapering band on a timed path, no beads, a braid summing to white; (3) words: pen driven reveal, ride and turn anchors, never blurred, exits to a curve or target; (4) nine moving coloured lights (mist glow, lake pool, rim, rays); (5) hue preserving highlights, whiteout drawing in to the sun; (6) bodies: joint positions over time, gather from a coil, colour wash, contact flare, motions: embrace, child lift, turning dance, prayer kneel, cupped hands; (7) camera motion blur; (8) arch: a colour per light, paths on water, size settings.
