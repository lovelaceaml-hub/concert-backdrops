Logline: a flame leaves two open hands and is carried flat out through seven worlds by seven ordinary people; a Word bursts from every touch. The fastest falls and the flame scatters; three words rebuild it, larger, in a child's hands. She carries it home to those first hands, Heavenly Mother's, and day breaks from them.
