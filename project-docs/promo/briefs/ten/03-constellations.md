# Film 03 Constellations: phase A (branch ten/03-constellations, head 69732f1, engine/songs/ten_03_constellations.py)
Logline: scattered stars are only scattered stars until a word of love joins them. Nine words draw nine lines between people made of stars; the lines become a web, the web closes into the arch, the arch turns and sweeps out a world, and day breaks behind it. The camera always revolves; sky above, a perfect mirror below, no hills.
Timeline: 0 star rush, at 1.0 stars snap into a woman's profile, orbit, her hand draws the first line (Greeting); 7.5 dive over the mirror, a walker thanks a kneeling worker (Gratitude); 15 meteor shower, a broken line mended, two hands touch (Apology, Inclusiveness); 22.5 one yields at a gate of stars, a listener, thoughts open into a galaxy (Concession, Respect); 30 a stair of stars, an elder roots for a climber, a hand reaches down (Encouragement, Consideration); 37.5 the burst (Compliment), breath at 40.6 with nine lights and Heavenly Mother as a far constellation from behind, at 42.6 the web ignites, fast pull-back; 45 the arch drawn dot to dot, revolves and sweeps out a globe the mirror completes; 52.5 sun breaks behind the globe, end card 55.3 to 60.
Wow: the alignment (1.0 and 10.0); the web chain reaction (42.6 to 45); the arch turning into a globe with sunrise behind (45 to 54).
Engine needs: (1) WORDS: points with fixed home places plus line, word, glyph and writing order, label, sharp mode while read; (2) CORE lines: near-plane clipping, depth of field, motion blur, travelling pulses, 50 thousand segments; (3) BODY: a constellation level of detail blending to the full plexus body, home places exposed, scale up to 300, feet at any height, sloped paths; (4) BODY motions: turn on the spot, bow head, rise from kneeling, step aside, climb, kneel and reach down, be pulled up, raise both arms, raise joined hands; (5) CORE world: no hills, calm true mirror, star dome turning about a chosen pole; (6) CORE arch: radius, yaw over time, line separate from lights, light positions readable; (7) CORE camera: focus and aperture tracks, strong motion blur, easing into reading keys; (8) a dark sphere mask that hides the sun and is mirrored; (9) medley sections as data.
