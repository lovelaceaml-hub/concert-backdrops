# Concert Backdrops: shared design rules for every song

The rules every video follows, in priority order. Each song's own design is in Designs/. A design may break a rule only if it explains why; Anthony decides. Reorganized 2026-11-13 with nothing removed.

## 1. THE MESSAGE (above every other rule)
Anthony, 2026-11-13: "Make sure each of the chats and the whole project understand that the mother's words of love that bring peace are the main theme and the main importance of this concert. That is the message we are trying to get across."

The concert exists to carry one message: the Words of Mother's Love bring peace. Every song, scene, effect, fragment and figure serves that message. The songs are the vehicle; the nine expressions are the point. In each video the audience should leave knowing which of Mother's words they saw, what each means in everyday life, and that practicing them brings peace. When a visual idea is beautiful but does not help that message, it gives way. When two choices compete, pick the one that makes the Words of Mother's Love clearer, warmer and more memorable.

## 2. Number one priority: know the campaign, then make it heavenly
Anthony, 2026-11-13: "Tell every thread to review the Mother's Love campaign thoroughly. That is the number one priority, and to think of magnificent, glorious, heavenly warming, strengthening effects from the love of a mother. Beautiful scenery, beautiful effects and shots, even the interactions. All of it should be beautiful, amazing, giving us hope for the kingdom of heaven. All of these need to be done."

- Study the campaign first: https://heavenlymotherslove.org/en/about , /en/checklist and its other English pages (videos, peacemaker stories, reviews, news), and Anthony's campaign app https://mothers-love.vercel.app (the nine expressions with everyday examples at /expressions, and the recognitions people have shared; never copy anyone's name). Know what each expression means, the situation it is for, and how real people practice it.
- Every scene, effect, camera shot and interaction should feel magnificent, glorious and heavenly: warm like a mother's embrace, strengthening like her encouragement, full of hope for the kingdom of heaven.
- Interactions between the people of light matter as much as the scenery: tender, dignified, joyful, clearly showing the expression being practiced.
- Each expression moment gets room to breathe: about 8 to 10 seconds with nothing competing.

## 6. The world
- Home scene: a still lake at night, a long mountain silhouette and its reflection, a starry sky, floating warm light particles, one warm star resting on the horizon. Each song may transform this world (snow, storm, sunrise, a field, a city of light) but starts from it or returns to it, so the show feels like one journey.
- Light is the one material: words, people, the nine lights and effects are all warm golden light and sparks.
- Engine features: skyline drawn from the music's loudness, ripples on the water at note onsets, sky glow that follows loudness, sparks that form words and fly up into lights (Engine/, VIDEO-HANDOFF.md).
- Each song has its own signature effects; no special effect is reused in another song (shared motifs like the lake and the arch are fine).
- Every song looks clearly different (Anthony, 2026-11-13: "The scenes should look different from each other"). The lake and the arch are the thread; the time of day, season, weather, colors, light, framing and texture change from song to song so no two videos (or promos) could be mistaken for each other. ## 7. Words on screen
- Opening: the song title large with the concert title beneath, about 5 seconds, then both rest small in the top left (concert title wording: section 12).
- Expressions: small spaced capital label (APOLOGY) above the phrase in a glowing warm-white serif, large enough to read from the back of the hall.
- Lyric fragments: never full lyrics. About 3 to 6 short fragments per song (usually 4 words or fewer) that carry the song's heart, each with its own special effect grown from the song's imagery; no two fragments in the concert appear the same way.
- Keep the bottom third of the screen calm: performers stand in front of it.

## 8. People (proposed 2026-11-13, awaiting Anthony's yes)
People made of light: formed from golden sparks with a soft outline and a glassy inner glow. Always clearly clothed (robes or modest everyday clothes, folds drawn in light). Heads are smooth glowing shapes with no facial features. Heavenly Mother only ever seen from behind or as hands, fully robed. A figure can gather from sparks, act out its expression, then dissolve into sparks that rise into the arch.

## 9. Timing and live playback
- The live lengths in section 4 rule. Recordings in this folder are references only; songs whose live arrangement differs need a rehearsal recording before final timing.
- Line times are estimates until checked by ear or measured against the performed version.
- How the video follows the live performance (an operator cueing each section, or straight playback timed to rehearsal) is an open decision (section 12). Every video ends on a calm hold that can last as long as needed, so a longer performance never goes black.

## 10. Never
No em dash or en dash. No lyrics copied into new files beyond the short fragments chosen for the screen. No realistic faces. No logos or watermarks. Song design sessions do not edit the MP3s, the Lyrics doc or the Visual Plan doc.
