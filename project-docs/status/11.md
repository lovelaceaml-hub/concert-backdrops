## Engine and tool notes (for the coordinator)
- tools/prepare_figure.py removes every row where more than 30 percent of pixels are bright, to strip a drawn floor. For a wide group of people (Mother with three children, a crowd of eleven) that erases the middle of their bodies, so only heads and feet showed on screen. Worked around here with the same steps minus that removal (my own copy, not committed). Suggested fix: only remove thin horizontal lines (a row run that is long and only a few pixels tall), or add a flag to skip it.
- Bright plates: people of light are added light, so on a near-white plate they need the plate gain lowered to about 0.55 to read.
