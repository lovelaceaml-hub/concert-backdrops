# Verdict

These eleven promos demonstrate a working rendering pipeline, but they do not yet demonstrate eleven unmistakably different scenes carrying the message that Mother's words bring peace. I would reject them as finished audience-facing promos until reading time, contrast, star history and the unresolved expression placements are corrected.
