## Creative treatment

The heavenly city, emerald Earth, progressively rising Zion, gathering nations,
real church architecture and dark concert ending remain the musical story.
Twenty-four selected camera compositions are newly authored. Travel, bank and
lens behavior are restrained so the worlds have depth and the movement reads.

The conductor has independent sapphire formal clothing with gold detail.
Choir and orchestra clothing also uses separate torso and seat geometry.
The conductor's body anticipates the musical gesture, the feet remain planted,
and the baton is attached to the evaluated hand. Abrupt source hand-score
changes are replaced by a continuous curve through the same musical cues.

Architectural light, haze and effects have distinct levels. Stage flares are
reduced where they obscure performers. The first crown composition reserves
space for the words; the sunrise is composed away from the reading area.
Fine moving waterways add foreground depth over the sea.
