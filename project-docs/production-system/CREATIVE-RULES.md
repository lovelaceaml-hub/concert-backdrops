# Creative and motion rules

## The meaning leads the picture

The campaign's nine practices concern greeting, gratitude, apology, inclusiveness, concession, respect, encouragement, consideration and compliment. Read the [official campaign](https://heavenlymotherslove.org/en/about) and [daily practice page](https://heavenlymotherslove.org/en/checklist), then use the approved project wording and colors in `engine/p3d/words.py`. The song catalog records historical expression continuity; new emphasis is a proposal until integrated deliberately.

The working dramatic pattern is: someone notices another person, chooses a loving action, the other person responds, and the light of that action changes their surroundings. This pattern can be intimate, joyful, solemn or grand. It should not make eleven identical films. Give each song its own world, season, palette, signature effect, pace and ending, while retaining shared campaign identity and the mirror-water language.

## People and acting

- Ordinary figures default to warm gold. The later all-song lessons specify sparkling blue choir/orchestra with gold instruments and books; that is an ensemble treatment, not a reason to turn every human blue.
- Use explicit casting. The established paired interactions use same-gender adults where required by the owner briefs; parent and child interactions retain their distinct roles.
- The later `PHASEB.md` clarification permits artistic facial features. Preserve the luminous, artistic human style; do not introduce photorealistic skin by accident. Heavenly Mother retains the owner's rear-view or hands-only portrayal.
- Structure a performance as preparation, action, response, contact, release and settling. Offset the torso, shoulder, elbow, wrist and fingers for a physical reason. Do not add the same periodic sway to every joint.
- Drive props and contact light from the final solved pose. Root travel, stride and planted feet must agree. A shared hand target needs a non-recursive evaluation path and a tested release.
- Use native evaluated foreground bodies; cheaper crowd caches are a separate detail tier. Crowd variation should create attentive groups, not random activity competing with the main event.

## Lessons from the new helping study

The original response solved a partly applied hand-on-heart gesture repeatedly. During its return, the left palm moved about 34.4 cm between two 60 fps samples. The isolated revision solves the complete endpoint, then blends complete poses through a smooth arrival, hold and return. The maximum return step in the measured interval is about 2.39 cm; prior shared contact is unchanged. This is evidence that a specific discontinuity was removed, not a universal natural-motion score. Repeat the numeric check with `tools/concert_review/response.py` and review the actual encoded movement.

A camera that clarifies the middle of an action can hide its beginning. The side-view probe separated the rising figures but concealed the helper during the initial contact. The revision retains the readable contact view, then changes view at original time 6.9 seconds while preserving screen order. Inspect the end of a camera move too: an otherwise good garden view put a bright flower patch across a head. The final endpoint was corrected before freezing.

## Camera and composition

- Establish who is acting and where they stand. Keep hands, heads and feet visible during help, greeting, giving and accepting.
- Hold a complete contact exchange in one shot unless a cut improves comprehension. Avoid crossing the action axis or reversing the perceived direction without a reason.
- Use moving foreground, people, shore, architecture and distant mountains at separate depths. A wide lens alone does not create scale.
- Begin and end important moves with controlled acceleration. Camera flight should lead the viewer along a meaningful action or light path.
- Protect the most important silhouette and word. Clouds, bright ribbons and particle streaks should pass around that subject instead of continuously covering it.

## Light, color and rendering

- Assign separate roles to people, expression light, environment, typography and atmosphere. One dominant event at a time is usually easier to read.
- Keep the campaign paint colors exact. The existing lighter glow treatment for dark blue/indigo labels is intentional.
- Set brightness expectations from the first review frame. Inspect local bright regions and subject separation, not only average frame luminance.
- Native rendering, controlled bloom, depth-aware focus and stable temporal sampling improve clarity. Do not advertise a performance multiplier until a representative benchmark measures it.
- Shutter samples stay inside the current shot. Avoid blending two camera cuts or two expressions into a single motion-blurred image.
- The approved p3d image is RGB8 with its historical finishing convention. The Blender RGB16 pipeline has a different precision and color contract. Do not silently switch one to the other under the same reference name.

## Text, identity and stage use

Use the exact existing campaign symbols and supplied emblem. The code-only workflow excludes the legacy generated raster people, plates and song figures. Existing permitted geometry, motion data and reference-based church models retain their provenance and attribution.

The November 19 addendum supplies future baseline behavior for the bottom-left corner logo, periodic song/theme titles and music-video pacing. Respect a more specific song design when it differs. Do not copy the dated promo end card or its venue information into every song as permanent stage furniture.

Allow adequate reading time and visual space. The full-song design normally grants longer expression reading periods than the short approved promo. A technical proof may omit large words while testing the acting, but a final concert film still needs its intended message treatment.

## Acceptance evidence

A polished image is necessary, but a performance must also survive motion review. Inspect the approach, contact, rise, release and ending at native size. Check a frame before and after every authored cut and every worker join. Compare final media, not only source PNGs. Keep automatic QA findings, a reviewer judgment and the owner's approval as separate records.
