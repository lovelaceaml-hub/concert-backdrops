## 1. THE MESSAGE (above every other rule)
Anthony, 2026-11-13: "Make sure each of the chats and the whole project understand that the mother's words of love that bring peace are the main theme and the main importance of this concert. That is the message we are trying to get across."

The concert exists to carry one message: the Words of Mother's Love bring peace. Every song, scene, effect, fragment and figure serves that message. The songs are the vehicle; the nine expressions are the point. In each video the audience should leave knowing which of Mother's words they saw, what each means in everyday life, and that practicing them brings peace. When a visual idea is beautiful but does not help that message, it gives way. When two choices compete, pick the one that makes the Words of Mother's Love clearer, warmer and more memorable.

Beautiful scenery, beautiful effects and shots, even the interactions. All of it should be beautiful, amazing, giving us hope for the kingdom of heaven. All of these need to be done."

- Interactions between the people of light matter as much as the scenery: tender, dignified, joyful, clearly showing the expression being practiced.
- Each expression moment gets room to breathe: about 8 to 10 seconds with nothing competing.

- Light is the one material: words, people, the nine lights and effects are all warm golden light and sparks.
- Engine features: skyline drawn from the music's loudness, ripples on the water at note onsets, sky glow that follows loudness, sparks that form words and fly up into lights (Engine/, VIDEO-HANDOFF.md).
- Each song has its own signature effects; no special effect is reused in another song (shared motifs like the lake and the arch are fine).

## 7. Words on screen
- Opening: the song title large with the concert title beneath, about 5 seconds, then both rest small in the top left (concert title wording: section 12).
- Expressions: small spaced capital label (APOLOGY) above the phrase in a glowing warm-white serif, large enough to read from the back of the hall.
- Lyric fragments: never full lyrics. About 3 to 6 short fragments per song (usually 4 words or fewer) that carry the song's heart, each with its own special effect grown from the song's imagery; no two fragments in the concert appear the same way.
- Keep the bottom third of the screen calm: performers stand in front of it.
