## What the sampled pictures show

**Promo evidence.** By the 39.400-second sample, a cut has brought the two people much closer. The prior title fades by 39.600. The speaker opens the conversational hand around 39.933 to 40.133; the open-palm pose is then largely held through 44.000. The listener faces the speaker with a comparatively small response. Lanterns, streaks and reflections keep changing, with a gentle change of view and no abrupt within-shot camera jump visible in these samples. The later title partly competes with the head region.

**Design inference.** The exchange reads because the people have a clear relationship and separated silhouettes. Much of the apparent activity comes from the surrounding world, rather than extensive joint animation. Preserve that hierarchy. The next improvement is one visible receiving response and a soft return from the held gesture. Constant motion in every joint would make the intention less clear.

**Song 11 evidence.** From 19.500 to 23.500, the full-body conductor remains legible against the layered city, mountains and mirror sea. In the consecutive frames, an outward arm opening is more visible at 23.600; a broad blue flare appears at 23.617; the view cuts wide at 23.633; curved paths become distinct from 23.650 to 23.733. The trails and reflections then occupy much more space while the smaller conductor establishes scale.

**Design inference.** Expansion from an individual to a large world response is a strong part of this visual language. The flare appears only one frame before the wide cut, masking much of the physical follow-through. A future release would read more causally if the wrist or baton arrival remains visible before the large flare and through the transition. These images do not establish whether the existing musical timing is correct, nor do they identify the camera's mathematical easing function.

Neither passage demonstrates a shared grip or load. They cannot certify hand interlocking, planted feet or the weight of an object. Those require their own performance proof.

## Three priorities for the next increment

1. **Give the receiving person an authored response.** Extend the paired actor recipe with attention, a delayed head/chest turn, one small answering gesture and a relaxed return. Use the promo's 39.933 to 40.133 opening as the clarity target. Keep the camera on both people through the response. Review without words first; the exchange should remain understandable and the feet should stay supported.

2. **Build one shared-prop contact performance.** Add a basket or carrying frame with explicit local hand targets, rather than positioning two independent hands near a floating object. Both grips establish before the lift; pelvis and knees lead the effort; torso, elbows, cloth and prop settle at different times; grips release after the load stops. The deliverable is a short, fully visible lift and set-down with contact evidence, not merely a new mesh. Preserve the warm gold people and keep the prop clear of their silhouettes.

3. **Expose the human release before the camera reveals its result.** Author preparation, wrist/baton arrival and follow-through as separate event times. Begin the traveling light at the actual posed hand or prop. For a transition comparable to study time 23.633, start by allowing roughly 6 to 12 readable frames of follow-through before a large flare or wide cut, then adjust after actual motion and audio review. Use consecutive-frame inspection around the event. Keep large text out of the hand/head area until the action has resolved.
