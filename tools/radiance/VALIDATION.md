The owner requested stronger camera and light direction, flying and aerial
tricks, passing word reflections and a loud finale. This revision adds banked

not pixel-identical engine optimizations.

## Music

The existing seven recordings are edited in order 09, 08, 04, 05, 11, 01, 03.
The edit uses pitch-preserving tempo adjustment toward 112 bpm and six 2.143
second crossfades with bass handoffs. No music was generated by a paid service.
The crescendo grows after 42 seconds; the final chord lands at 60.2 seconds.
The source climax is 8.60 dB above the middle passage by RMS measurement.
Stereo-linked soft limiting controls peaks, followed by a brief final release.

The old quiet-final-second check has deliberately been replaced: the new check
requires a strong climax and a clean last 10 ms release. This implements the
owner's loud-ending direction. No listening audition is claimed; level and timing
measurements cannot establish musical flow or emotional impact.
