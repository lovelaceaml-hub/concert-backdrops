# Consideration proof: review notes

## What changed artistically

The prior sparse demonstration established sharper rendering, but its people and light effects had little shared purpose. This proof centers a complete action: a person offers a hand, the seated person accepts, they share the effort of rising, then acknowledge each other. Only afterward does a pink light awaken the surrounding garden. Three shots have separate roles: discovery, an uninterrupted view of the action, and the world reveal.

Warm gold people retain the approved promo's ordinary human appearance. The dark mirror water preserves their silhouettes. Vegetation is placed away from the pair's immediate space, the used seat becomes dimmer after the rise, and text appears in the final wide view. The permanent corner treatment is the existing logo alone. The Consideration capsule takes its color from `words.EXPRESSIONS` rather than a replacement pink.

The backdrop and motion remain intentionally stylized. This proof should not be described as fully realistic human simulation or as completion of every concert model.

## Source and motion

The 6.5-second paired CMU capture is reused, not newly captured or wholly hand animated. The scene adds a retargeting contact overlay, hand detail, controlled hand emission, an authored ending settlement and acknowledgement, camera staging, native environmental models and light choreography. These are new scene contributions. The human mesh, skinning, cloth solver, renderer and campaign graphics are existing project work.

The old contact source had a visible numerical palm offset, around 0.1 to 0.27 m during portions of the shared effort. The current shared-target overlay brought the sampled palm-center gaps to 6.3 mm at 5.3 seconds, 10.8 mm at 6.0, 8.8 mm at 6.5 and 3.5 mm at 7.0. Approach and release intentionally have larger gaps. Palm-center distance does not establish correct finger interlocking or physical force, so the actual rendered gesture remains the primary evidence.

The source capture ends in a departing step. An isolated 1.2-second transition now settles the actors at their final captured horizontal root positions and turns them toward each other. This resolves the indefinite raised-foot hold seen in the first still pass. The settle is authored, not part of the original capture; close inspection of its foot trajectory is still appropriate.

## Observed still revisions

The first native 1920x1080 images showed the right human scale and recognizable help, but a broad warm light veil reduced separation; the seat and path emitted too much light; and tall plants were visible through the translucent figures. The next pass removed that broad scene light, reduced haze and mist, lowered seat and path emission, reduced hand emission while keeping geometric density, and settled the final body poses. The final composition adjustment lowers and moves plants away from the interaction, fades the used seat and restores the logo-only corner baseline.

The 16.5-second wide view shows a completed garden, the people still present, a pink thread into the willow and the exact expression words. This is a resolved ending designed for the proof. It does not carry the immense architectural scale of Song 11; that scale can be added with the existing distant architecture and crowd library after the close human scene is sound.

## Known practical limits

- The figures are made of emitted points, so they remain partly translucent. Careful background placement is still required.
- Dense overlapping palms can look brighter than individual fingers. Lower emission improves visibility but does not make a fully opaque hand surface.
- Cloth uses the existing fixed-step simulation and cached snapshots. It is not a new high-resolution garment solver.
- The plants are deliberately geometric light motifs. The willow is a stylized branching canopy; the flowers are not botanical reconstructions.
- The bridge variant is a geometric asset. Walking on its height profile needs a separate contact-aware performance.
- The audio cue at relative 11.5 seconds is informed by the original Song 07 scene timeline. It is not labeled as an ear-verified transient.
- The preview envelope supplies required legacy-renderer fields without claiming to be an instrument analysis.

## Technical review is separate

Native resolution, distinct frame count, exact timing, color tags, source hashes and cache isolation protect reproduction and delivery. They cannot certify tenderness, meaningful action or beauty. Final acceptance should include the actual motion through offer, grip, rise, release, settlement and world response, with the prepared audio, plus comparison against the approved promo's emotional and visual language.
